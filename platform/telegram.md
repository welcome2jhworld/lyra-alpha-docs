# Telegram

## For agents

- Lets you: Know what reaches the user on Telegram and how often.
- Tools: none
- Limit: At most 3 messages a week from Lyra; decisions are not counted.

## Link

1. In the app, open Settings. On the Telegram row, press Connect.
2. Telegram opens the Lyra bot with `/start <code>`.
3. The bot confirms the link.

## Commands

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/start` | Links this chat, or confirms it is linked |
| `/status` | Your open pods and this week's message count |
| `/stop` | Unlinks this chat |

- Any other text goes to Lyra as a chat message.
- Buttons on a decision answer it in place.

## What reaches you

- Decisions that need you.
- A Risk Agent block that matters.
- The weekly letter.
- At most 3 messages a week, decisions not counted.

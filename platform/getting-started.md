# Getting started

## For agents

- Lets you: Walk a new user from sign-in to a first paper pod.
- Tools: `build_research`, `build_run`, `pods_templates`, `pods_propose`, `pods_fund`
- Limit: Free: 3 checks a day, 1 pod, 100 credits for 30 days.
- Limit: A pod starts only from the user's Start.

## Steps

1. Sign in at the Lyra app.
2. Lyra asks for an idea. Type one sentence, such as "Gen Z is drinking less".
3. Lyra may ask by when, and what would prove it wrong.
4. Lyra checks it and reads the result back: "Good Idea. It held in 10 of 15 past cases."
5. Lyra shows a pod draft: holdings, sizes, loss levels.
6. Press Start. The pod opens on paper with real prices.
7. Link Telegram to hear from Lyra when you are needed.

## No idea yet

- Lyra shows prebuilt pods with their paper record next to the S&P 500 and BTC.
- Open Research, Public Research: Lyra's 3 ideas a day, each with Check and Start on paper.
- Pick one and press Start.

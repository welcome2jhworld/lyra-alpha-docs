# Paper fund

## For agents

- Lets you: Read a paper fund's pods and record.
- Tools: `pods_fund`, `pods_templates`
- Limit: Free is paper only, 1 pod, 30 days.

## Facts

- Hyperliquid paper: $10,000 per fund.
- Alpaca paper: the equity of your linked Alpaca paper account, read live.
- No Alpaca paper account linked: stocks can't trade on paper.
- Prices: real.
- Agents: the same Analyst, Trader and Risk Agent as Live.
- Perps: simulated fills on Hyperliquid prices.
- US stocks: trade in your Alpaca paper account after you link it. [Connect Alpaca](alpaca.md).
- Every pod is shown next to S&P 500 and BTC.
- Paper is always labelled paper.

## Moving to Live

- Live opens a second fund. Bring your Paper pods, or start fresh.
- [Paper and Live](paper-and-live.md).

# FAQ

## For agents

- Lets you: Answer common questions with a link to the full page.
- Tools: `docs_search`

## Does Lyra trade without me?

- On paper, pods trade inside their limits once you press Start.
- Real money moves only after you press Start on a Live fund.
- [Paper and Live](paper-and-live.md).

## Can my AI agent place trades?

- No. Agents read and propose. [What agents can't do](security.md).

## Why "Good Idea" and not "Awesome Idea"?

- A hand-picked basket grades at most Good Idea.
- Awesome Idea needs a screened universe, the sealed period and CPCV. [How a check grades](grading.md).

## Why did my check count go up when I changed one number?

- A changed constant is a new version of the same family. Every version is a trial.

## What happens when I run out of credits?

- Chat and research stop until the next period or a pack. Pods keep trading. The Risk Agent keeps watching.

## Can I short US stocks?

- On paper, yes, when Alpaca marks the name shortable and easy to borrow.
- On live, no. Shorts go on Hyperliquid perps.

## What if my Alpaca connection breaks?

- Lyra marks it revoked once, tells you, and leaves positions where they are.

## Does Lyra see the future in backtests?

- No. Every read is point in time. [Lyra Data Engine](data-engine.md).

## Which markets?

- Hyperliquid perps (crypto and HIP-3) and US stocks and ETFs through your Alpaca account.

## How do I cancel?

- Plans are billed through Whop. Manage or cancel the membership there.
- Free pauses its paper pods after 30 days.

# Research

## For agents

- Lets you: Know where ideas and research runs live in the app.
- Tools: `build_research`, `build_runs`, `build_run`
- Limit: Public Research ideas are read in the app; there is no MCP tool for them.

## Two tabs

| Tab | Holds | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Public Research | Lyra's daily ideas, 3 a day, by day | [Ideas](ideas.md) |
| Your Research | Your own views and their research runs | [Your Research](your-research.md) |

## From either tab

- Check: run the check on the idea. [How a check grades](grading.md).
- Start on paper: open it as a pod in your Paper fund.
- Real money only after you press Start on a Live fund. [Paper and Live](paper-and-live.md).

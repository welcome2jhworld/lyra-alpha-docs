# Paper and Live

## For agents

- Lets you: Explain the two fund modes and what it takes to go live.
- Tools: `pods_fund`
- Limit: Live needs Plus or above.
- Limit: No tool moves a pod to live or moves money. The user does it in the app.

## The two modes

```figure paper-live
Paper: Hyperliquid $10,000 per fund; Alpaca your linked paper account's equity → You press Start → Live: your Hyperliquid and Alpaca accounts
```

| | Paper | Live |
|---|---|---|
| Hyperliquid | $10,000 per fund, simulated fills | your account, your deposit, Lyra's agent key |
| Alpaca | your linked Alpaca paper account's equity, read live; none linked, no stocks | your Alpaca live account |
| Prices | real | real |
| Plans | all | Plus and above |
| Shorts on Alpaca | yes | no |

## Rules

- A fund is Paper or Live. You can hold one of each on Plus and above.
- Every pod starts on paper.
- Going live opens a Live fund. You choose: bring your Paper pods, or start fresh.
- A Live fund needs at least $150 deposited.
- Each live pod needs at least $150.
- A live fund under $300 runs one pod.
- The free plan is paper only and ends after 30 days.

## What never happens without you

- No pod opens without your Start.
- No money moves between accounts without you.
- No agent, MCP tool or Telegram message can do either.
